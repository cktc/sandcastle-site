Who can apply

You can apply if you:

  • are 18 or over
  • are an Australian resident (citizen, permanent resident, or visa holder with work rights)
  • have regular income from work, paid into an Australian bank account in your name. Casual and shift workers can apply if bank data shows a steady pattern.
  • have an Australian bank account with online banking
  • can show photo ID (driver licence, passport or Medicare card) at the full application

Who we cannot lend to, and why

We cannot lend to you if government payments are your only or main income. A repayment on top could leave you short for rent and food. Government payments can count as extra income, assessed with your other income.

We also cannot lend if we cannot confirm you can repay without hardship. That protects you.

What a decline will tell you

If we say no, we tell you the main reason, such as: your income could not be confirmed, the repayment looked too high against your spending, or an identity check failed. We also say what to do next, like a smaller amount or longer term, if that would help.
