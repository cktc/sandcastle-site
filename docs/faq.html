Questions and answers

How much will I actually pay back?

You see the total in dollars before you apply, and again in your contract. See our worked example on the rates page.

Will you cut my limit?

No. Your loan amount is set in your contract. We cannot reduce it or call it back once you accept, as long as you keep to the contract.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Tell us early. Call 1300 000 000. You may pay a late fee of [$ amount, or None] and interest on the unpaid amount. We will talk through options, such as a new date. You can go to AFCA if you disagree.

Is this a payday loan?

It is a personal loan of $2,001 to $5,000 over 6 to 18 months, with equal repayments. You repay it with interest and fees. It is not an advance on your next pay.

Why do you need my bank login?

You never give us your password. You use Basiq (placeholder) to share your transactions, read-only, for 90 days. We use them to confirm income and spending. We cannot move money. You can withdraw consent any time.

What do you check?

Your income, expenses and bank data, and your credit history where we use it.

Can I pay off early?

Yes. There is no early-repayment fee, and interest stops when you pay off.

What if I am a casual or shift worker?

You can apply. We read your earnings from your bank data, quiet weeks included, and set the repayment from the quieter weeks. Your pays do not need to match.

What if you say no?

We tell you why and what you can do next.

How long until I know?

You get a decision within 10 minutes of linking your bank, in business hours. A decision, not an approval. We tell you the decision and the reasons.

I am struggling to pay. Who do I talk to?

Call 1300 000 000, [Days and hours, e.g. Mon to Fri 9am to 5pm AEST]. You will talk to a person.

How do I make a complaint?

Call or write to us first. If you are not happy, you can go to AFCA, free and independent: 1800 931 678, afca.org.au.

What if my decision takes longer than 10 minutes?

Then the establishment fee is waived. The clock starts when your bank link completes, in business hours. It is a decision we promise, not an approval; every application is assessed.

Can I change my mind after the money arrives?

Yes. Repay the full amount within 48 hours of it landing and you owe nothing, no interest and no fee.

What is the good-payer rate?

After six on-time repayments you are eligible for a lower rate on any later loan with us. Eligible means we assess you again; it is not automatic.
