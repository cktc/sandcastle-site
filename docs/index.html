Many lenders judge the shape of your pay, not whether you can repay

Many lenders want a regular wage. A casual roster, a second job, a few nights driving, and the answer is often no. The shape of your pay is not the same as your capacity to repay it.

We read up to 12 months of your bank earnings, quiet weeks included, and set the repayment from those. Every application is assessed. If the answer is no, we tell you why and what would change it.

Then the bill. The car fails its roadworthy. A tooth cracks. The bond is due before the old one comes back. In a rush it is easy to take the first offer and learn the real cost later. A loan should show its full cost before you sign.

Without the total, you can sign for a price you only learn later.

With it, you pay one bill and know the end date.

Four things included

  • Payday match. Repayments land on your payday, and you get one free date move a year.
  • Early exit, no fee. Pay it off whenever you like and pay nothing extra. Clear the $3,100 example at month 9 and you save about $414.
  • Your amount is yours. No limit cuts or recalls while you keep to the contract.
  • Good-payer rate. After six on-time repayments you are eligible for a lower rate on any later loan.

A surprise bill is stressful. You should not have to rush.

Questions people ask first

Can the amount change after I sign?

No. Your loan amount is set in your contract. We cannot reduce it or call it back once you accept, as long as you keep to the contract.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Tell us early. Call 1300 000 000. You may pay a late fee of [$ amount, or None] and interest on the unpaid amount. We will talk through options, such as a new date. You can go to AFCA if you disagree.

Is this a payday loan?

It is a personal loan of $2,001 to $5,000 over 6 to 18 months, with equal repayments. You repay it with interest and fees. It is not an advance on your next pay.

What if my decision takes longer than 10 minutes?

Then the establishment fee is waived. The clock starts when your bank link completes, in business hours. It is a decision we promise, not an approval. Every application is assessed.
