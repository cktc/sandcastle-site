How it works

  1. 1

    Check your rate

    Pick $2,001 to $5,000 and 3 to 12 months. Answer four quick questions. Connect your bank, read-only. You see your rate, repayment and total. Link your bank in business hours and you have a decision in 10 minutes, or the $400 fee is waived. A decision, not an approval.

  2. 2

    Choose your repayments

    Every repayment is the same. You pick the term that suits your pay. Pay off early whenever you like: no early-repayment fee, and interest stops.

  3. 3

    Get your money

    If you are approved, you read your contract and accept it. We pay the money into your bank account.

How we assess you

Every application is assessed. We check your income, expenses and bank data to confirm you can repay without hardship. Approval is not guaranteed.

Your rate is set from your income and spending, and your credit history where we use it.

Change your mind

Repay the full amount within 48 hours of the money landing and you owe nothing. No interest and no fee.

If we say no

We tell you why, in plain words, and what you can do next. See what a decline tells you.
